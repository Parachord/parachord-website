Plugging the big idea

Stop chasing music across platforms.

Given metadata about a song, album, or playlist, Parachord finds the best available source for you and just plays it.

Features

Many Music Sources

Connect your favorite streaming services and local libraries, then prioritize them however you like. Parachord seamlessly switches sources so your music never stops.

Friends & Listen Along

See what friends are playing — in whatever apps they use — browse their libraries, and listen along in real-time. Music is better together.

Shuffleupagus AI

AI-powered recommendations from the engines you choose — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or all at once. Your taste, amplified.

Service Interoperability

Someone shared a Spotify link but you use Apple Music? Parachord translates between services so you can play anything from anywhere.

Smartlinks & Embeds

Share a song that opens in the listener’s preferred service. One link, every platform, no dead ends.

Sync Library & Favorites

Sync favorites and libraries from your existing services. Your carefully curated collection, unified in one place.

The wider ecosystem

Built as open, decoupled layers.

parachord:// deep links

Any website can hand a tracklist to Parachord with one click — it resolves and plays from your top-ranked source.

Own Your Playlists

Export in standard XSPF format that works forever. No lock-in, no expiry, no platform holding your collection hostage.

MCP Server

Let Claude and other agents browse, queue, and control your music through the Parachord MCP server.

Achordion Community

A modern web community on ListenBrainz data — every Play button deep-links straight back into Parachord.
